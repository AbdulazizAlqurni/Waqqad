The symbol, ready for your sign-off.

At rest it is the Lift mark: three navy tiles and the amber diamond above them. When it moves, only the diamond changes. It becomes an upright amber flame with a pale core, burns while you stay, and settles back into the diamond. Everything on this page is built from the same files you will receive.

Changed since round 3

  • The flame stands upright and sways a little either side as it burns. It no longer leans left.
  • The pale core (#FFE3A8) is part of the flame, and only in motion.
  • Hover changes only the diamond. The effect you saw, with every diamond growing and overlapping, was a bug: the Ember core card shared a name with round 2's dropped Core spark and picked up its hover. That leftover motion is gone.
  • The other flame forms are retired. The flame you marked Favourite in Ember is the flame inside Ignite.

The set at restالثابت

The static symbol for print, documents, stamps and anywhere that cannot move. It never shows the flame.

3

Constructionالبناء

Four squares on a grid turned 45°. The top square lifts out and turns amber. The dashed amber frame is the clear space: nothing else may enter it.

Colour

Navy#0B1F3Atiles, ground
Amber#F2A93Bthe spark only
Pale core#FFE3A8flame, motion only
White#FFFFFFtiles on navy

Rules

Geometry
Tile 24, gap 5, corner radius 2.2, turned 45°. The spark lifts 10 along the grid's diagonal.
Clear space
Half the spark's width on every side.
Smallest size
16 px on screen (favicon), 6 mm in print.
One colour
All navy, or all white on dark. The lift still shows which tile is the spark.
Never
Show the flame in print, rotate the mark, recolour the tiles, or use the spark on its own as the logo.
